Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PurePlay for YouTube ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information.

Summary: PurePlay for YouTube does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension adheres strictly to a zero-data-collection model:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, address, or IP address).
  • We do not monitor, record, or track your browsing activity or YouTube watch history.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising scripts, or cookies.
  • We do not maintain external servers or databases.

2. How the Extension Operates

PurePlay for YouTube operates purely on client-side logic executed locally within your Chrome browser:

  • Audio Track Selection: Interacts with the YouTube video player on the loaded page to select the original audio track instead of automated translations.
  • Player Defaults: Toggles YouTube's native subtitle and autoplay controls on video initialization.
  • Playback Speed: Reads and modifies the playback rate (and briefly displays an on-screen 1.5x / 1x callout) when you trigger the extension action icon.
  • Local Feature Preferences: Uses Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) and action context menu solely to save your on/off toggle states for Enforce Original Audio Track, Disable Subtitles, and Disable Autoplay locally on your device.

None of this data leaves your local device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not transmit any data to third-party services or vendors. Any interaction occurs directly between your browser and YouTube (Google LLC) under Google's standard Privacy Policy.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
