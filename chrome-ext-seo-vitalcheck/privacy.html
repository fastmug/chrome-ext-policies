Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SEO VitalCheck ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information.

Summary: SEO VitalCheck does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension adheres strictly to a zero-data-collection model:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, IP address, or device IDs).
  • We do not monitor, record, or track your general browsing activity, visited tabs, or search queries.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, telemetry, tracking pixels, advertising scripts, or cookies.
  • We do not operate external backend servers, remote databases, or cloud processing pipelines.

2. How the Extension Operates & Required Permissions

SEO VitalCheck operates purely through client-side logic executed locally within your Chrome browser. It requires only the minimum necessary permissions to fulfill its diagnostic purpose:

  • Target Website Access (host_permissions: <all_urls>): The extension performs HTTP requests exclusively to the specific website URLs and their corresponding robots.txt files that you choose to configure and test. These requests inspect HTTP headers, status codes, canonical tags, meta robots directives, page titles, and heading tags locally in your browser session. Your routine web browsing outside of running explicit checks is never accessed, intercepted, or logged.
  • Googlebot User-Agent Simulation (declarativeNetRequest): Used locally to modify request headers during diagnostic runs by setting the Googlebot User-Agent string. This ensures that the scanned web server responds with the exact headers, indexability directives, and content served to Google's search crawler.
  • Local Storage (storage): All configured test URLs, custom check rules, and previous test run history are stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your computer.

None of this data is ever sent to or accessible by the developer or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not communicate with any third-party APIs, CDNs, or external tracking services. All diagnostic checks are executed directly between your local browser and the target URLs you designate.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
