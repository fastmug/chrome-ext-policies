Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Active Sleeper ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information.

Summary: Active Sleeper does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, form content, or keystrokes. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension adheres strictly to a zero-data-collection model:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, IP address, or device identifiers).
  • We do not monitor, record, log, or track your browsing activity, visited URLs, or tab history.
  • We do not read, intercept, or capture user keystrokes, form inputs, text entries, or web page content.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, telemetry frameworks, advertising scripts, or cookies.
  • We do not operate or communicate with external servers, cloud services, or remote databases.

2. How the Extension Operates & Uses Permissions

Active Sleeper operates purely on client-side routines executed locally within the context of the user-selected tab:

  • Active Tab Activation (activeTab): Only when you explicitly click the extension's toolbar icon, temporary permission is granted to interact with your currently active tab to engage or disengage the keep-alive engine.
  • In-Page Keep-Alive Routines (scripting): Injects localized, non-destructive execution logic designed solely to maintain session continuity:
    • Visibility & Focus Continuity: Emulates active document visibility and focus state within the page context to prevent idle timeout triggers.
    • Anti-Throttling Audio: Generates a local, silent, inaudible audio buffer to prevent the browser engine from discarding or deeply throttling background tabs.
    • Non-Mutating Activity Pulses: Dispatches periodic synthetic events (such as non-printable Shift key signals and sub-pixel micro-movements) to reset DOM inactivity timers without modifying or interacting with form inputs, text areas, or editable elements.
  • Local State Persistence (storage): Remembers the active/inactive toggle state locally across browser restarts using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local).

None of this operational logic inspects or extracts your private page content, and no data ever leaves your local machine.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Activity

Active Sleeper is entirely self-contained. It makes zero outgoing network requests and does not transmit any information to third parties, analytics providers, or external endpoints.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
