Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pickshot ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information.

Summary: Pickshot does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension adheres strictly to a zero-data-collection model:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, address, or IP address).
  • We do not monitor, record, or track your browsing activity or search history.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, advertising scripts, or cookies.
  • We do not maintain external servers or databases.

2. How the Extension Operates

Pickshot operates purely on client-side logic executed locally within your Chrome browser:

  • Screenshot & Annotation Tools: Captures selected areas, visible viewports, or full-page screenshots, or loads local/clipboard images upon direct user request, and opens them in a local client-side editor for cropping, drawing, highlighting, blurring, and text annotation.
  • Color Picker & Pixel Loupe: Samples pixel colors from the active tab when triggered by the user and copies the selected HEX color code directly to your local clipboard.
  • Measure Elements: Inspects the pixel dimensions of hovered webpage containers and elements locally in the active tab when triggered by the user and copies the selected dimensions (e.g., 400x300) directly to your local clipboard.
  • Clean & Print View: Creates a temporary local print preview where you can click to hide unwanted page elements before printing or saving as a PDF via Chrome's native print dialog.
  • Local Storage: Remembers your local preferences (such as default image format, filename pattern, and download directory preference) and temporarily caches active screenshot edits exclusively on your device using Chrome's local storage and IndexedDB.

None of this data leaves your local device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not transmit any data to third-party services or vendors. Any interaction occurs directly within your browser on the webpage you choose to capture or inspect. The extension contains a voluntary external link (Ko-fi for optional creator appreciation in the settings page) that opens in a new tab under its respective privacy policy.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
