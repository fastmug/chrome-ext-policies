Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Three Percent ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles user information.

Summary: Three Percent does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or user identifiers. Everything runs 100% locally in your browser side panel.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Extension adheres strictly to a zero-data-collection model:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, email, address, or IP address).
  • We do not monitor, record, or track your browsing activity, visited tabs, or search history.
  • We do not use tracking pixels, analytics SDKs, telemetry, advertising scripts, or cookies.
  • We do not maintain external servers, cloud databases, or remote logging infrastructure.

2. How the Extension Operates

Three Percent operates purely on client-side logic executed locally within your Chrome browser side panel:

  • Calculations & Projections: All percentage calculations (including proportions, percentage deltas, tax/VAT additions, discounts, rule of three, and monthly pacing projections) are computed on-the-fly directly inside your local browser instance.
  • Local State Persistence: Remembers your active calculation inputs and values exclusively on your local device using Chrome's local storage API (chrome.storage.local) so your workflow is preserved between sessions.
  • Minimal Permissions: The extension only requests the sidePanel and storage permissions. It deliberately omits permissions like activeTab, tabs, and scripting, ensuring it cannot view, access, or modify webpage content or browser history.

None of your calculation data or input values ever leave your local device.

3. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

The Extension is completely self-contained. It initiates zero external network requests and does not transmit any data to third-party services, analytics vendors, or remote APIs.

4. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices in future versions, revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
